Motivation for Hudaia
الدافع وراء هداية

Guidance · Knowledge · Community — هداية · علم · مجتمع

Inspired by the Quran's message of guidance, Hudaia seeks to help people learn, reflect, connect with knowledgeable teachers, and serve one another with mercy and wisdom.

Our inspiration | مصدر إلهامنا

فَإِمَّا يَأْتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّي هُدًى فَمَنِ اتَّبَعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا يَضِلُّ وَلَا يَشْقَىٰ

“When guidance comes to you from Me, whoever follows My guidance will neither go astray nor suffer.” — Surah Ta-Ha (طه), 20:123

Hudaia is not the source of guidance. Our purpose is to help people discover, understand, and follow the guidance of Allah through the Quran, beneficial knowledge, and community.
